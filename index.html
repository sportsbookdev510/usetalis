How it works

Fifteen concepts, one card each. Every card opens the detail in the docs.

All concepts

  • 01Asset Backing

    The Stock Token is locked in the series vault to back the payoff. No margin, no funding rate, no liquidation engine.

  • 02Asset Distinction

    Positions are created from Stock Tokens and carry Income or Upside exposure. The protocol token is separate from them.

  • 03Defined Cap

    K = P0 × (1 + cap). Upside pays nothing at or below K and the amount above it.

  • 04Income Position

    Accept an upside cap for a defined period. Receive the premium paid by the upside buyer. Underlying downside exposure remains.

  • 05Market Epochs

    Each epoch records a starting price and a cap. Monthly, settling on the third Friday; 28 or 35 days each.

  • 06Market Structure

    One Stock Token, one epoch, one cap per series. Income earns the premium; Upside buys the move above the cap.

  • 07Oracle Settlement

    A 30-minute average of the Chainlink feed inside the regular session sets the value allocated to each side.

  • 08Outcome Scenarios

    Start $250, cap $262.50. At $200 or $250 Upside pays nothing. At $300 Upside pays $37.50; Income keeps $262.50 plus the premium.

  • 09Position Redemption

    One Income position plus one Upside position of the same series merge back into one Stock Token at any time, free.

  • 10Premium Exchange

    The upside buyer pays a premium in a descending-clock auction. The proceeds are credited to the Income side.

  • 11Protocol Revenue

    One fee: 5 % of gross auction proceeds, in USDG. Split, merge and settlement claims are free.

  • 12Risk Perspective

    Income still bears the downside of the Stock Token. The upside buyer can lose the premium paid.

  • 13Tokenized Markets

    Stock Tokens on Robinhood Chain. A token with a Chainlink feed and a USDG pool can be listed.

  • 14Token Utility

    TALIS is live on Robinhood Chain. Initial supply 1,000,000,000, no emissions; phase-1 utility subject to final design.

  • 15Upside Position

    Pay a premium for exposure above a defined level. The position can expire without a payoff.
